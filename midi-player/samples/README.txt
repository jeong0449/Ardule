=== Finding freely distributable MIDI files ===

Additional MIDI files suitable for redistribution can be found at the
Mutopia Project:

https://www.mutopiaproject.org/

Mutopia provides downloadable MIDI files for many classical works.
Licensing varies by individual item, so please check the "Copyright"
information on each piece's page before redistributing a MIDI file.
Many items are explicitly marked Public Domain.

Another source is the Classical Archives Free MIDI collection:

https://www.classicalarchives.com/prs/free.html

The MIDI files on that page are explicitly permitted for commercial and
non-commercial use, subject to the attribution requirements stated there.
